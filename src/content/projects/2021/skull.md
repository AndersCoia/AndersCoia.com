## Overview
I wanted to explore a low‑poly animation style that feels simple, cheerful, and intentionally “crafted”—something that could support a goofy story without needing heavy realism.

## Synopsis
A skull rests on a stone casket inside a mausoleum at the top of a mountain. A gust of wind knocks it loose and it rolls down the mountain, picking up speed before coming to a stop at the base. As night falls, a faint glow appears in its eye sockets—the skull “wakes up,” realises it’s far from home, spots the mausoleum in the distance, and uses a hanging vine to launch itself back. It hops into place just as sunrise arrives, returning to stillness.

## Visual Direction
- **Style:** Low poly, clean forms, simple shapes, and readable silhouettes.
- **Tone:** Goofy and cheerful—especially challenging at night, where colour can easily become gloomy. The plan was to keep colours lighter/less saturated and use a bright moon to maintain that mood.

## Technique & Workflow
- **Character rig:** A minimal rig with two main bones (skull + lower jaw) to keep movement expressive and efficient.
- **Interaction rigging:** Planned rigging for the branch and vine so the “launch” moment feels physical and purposeful. I explored whether physics would help the vine feel more natural, balanced against workload.
- **Planning:** Storyboards were used to lock in pacing, beats, and shot-to-shot clarity before animation.

## Outcome
A complete concept-to-production workflow: inspiration gathering, script and storyboard planning, asset creation, rigging approach, and a clear path to animation and render.

## Role
Concept, storyboarding, modelling, rigging plan, animation production.
